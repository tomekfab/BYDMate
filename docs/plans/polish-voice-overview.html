Why this approach

  • Reuse the Sherpa runtime: avoids adding a second speech runtime.
  • Parakeet for development: you chose it after a comparison on 16 synthetic clips and 2 of your recordings. It runs about 1 GB of memory and decodes much faster than the Whisper models that scored as well. Whisper turbo scored higher but is larger and slower; it stays a fallback candidate.
  • Keep speech offline: online recognition does not meet your choice.
Main risk: accuracy and speed in your car

Parakeet misses some words, and neither accuracy nor speed has been measured in the car. Test Polish recordings and then the parked Leopard 5 without internet. Target a response within three seconds after a short command on a warm model. A wrong vehicle action blocks release.

Remaining gates for Parakeet
  • Accuracy gate: still failing, 15 of 16 synthetic clips. It drops the end of temperatury. On your recordings it heard szywerdach for szyberdach. The test is unchanged and still red.
  • Loads and keeps negation: the pinned model runs on the Android 14 emulator and every negative command kept nie. This is a compatibility check, not an accuracy pass, and not a grammar safety result.
  • Language detection untested: Polish cannot be forced. Short, noisy and non-Polish speech get checked later, and a doubtful result must never widen what a command matches.
  • Car checks: speed, memory, noise and repeated sessions on the head unit are not measured.
  • Licence: NVIDIA's CC BY 4.0 needs visible credit and a note that the model was converted to int8. Terms of the third-party conversion are unstated and unresolved. Nothing is mirrored.
