Why this approach

  • Reuse Whisper support: avoids adding a second speech runtime.
  • Start with tiny: lower initial resource demand; test base if accuracy falls short.
  • Keep speech offline: online recognition does not meet your choice.
Main risk: accuracy and speed in your car

Neither has been measured yet. Test Polish recordings and then the parked Leopard 5 without internet. Target a response within three seconds after a short command on a warm model. A wrong vehicle action blocks release.
