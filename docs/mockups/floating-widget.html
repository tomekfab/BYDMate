// Interactions
TAP
Opens BYDMate (MainActivity). The widget automatically hides while the app is in the foreground and returns in the background.
DRAG
Free dragging. An 8dp threshold distinguishes a tap from a drag. While the finger moves, the bottom of the screen shows zone-trash.
DROP in zone-trash
The widget hides and the Settings switch turns off. To restore it, turn it on in Settings.
Zone-trash appears only during a drag — at rest the screen is clean, with no overlays.
Position zone-trash: bottom centre (an intuitive “sweep down” gesture), 72×72 px, red halo.
Magnetic zone: when the widget is inside the circle, it is marked as “will be removed” (grey/smaller), trash icon enlarges.

Border colour semantics (by the worse value of SOC and 12V):
green — SOC ≥ 30% and 12V ≥ 12.5
yellow — SOC 15–30% or 12V 12.0–12.5
red — SOC < 15% or 12V < 12.0
grey — DiPlus unavailable / no data

Fonts: Manrope for labels, JetBrains Mono for numbers.
Corners: RoundedCornerShape(12dp) — identical to CompactCard on the dashboard.
Background: #16161a (CardSurface), 0 6px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.5) shadow.
