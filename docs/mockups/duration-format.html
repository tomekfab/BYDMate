BYDMate — trip duration format

The field appears in two places: the dashboard (recent trips) and the trip list. The current format is 50 min / 1 h 20 min. The short minute marker should be changed to “min”. Review the options and choose one.

Option 1 — spaces: “50 min” / “1 h 20 min”
